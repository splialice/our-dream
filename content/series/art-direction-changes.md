Two games, two records of a studio changing how a game looked. **Borderlands** made the call late, with the project roughly three-quarters finished, and left a 2010 conference talk, a contemporaneous report and dated interviews. **Team Fortress 2** moved away from realism across several abandoned versions, and left a 2007 technical paper, a slide deck and a developer video from the year it shipped.

Neither group is a guide to drawing the style. Both are about what can be proved: who said what, when, and which documents survive. Read a group from its first article, and use [Answers](/questions/) when you came with one specific question.
